## 🧭 What I care about

A lot of what I build is for people the usual apps skip over: gig workers and informal traders who need their money tools to work offline, kids learning to read, and anyone who finds starting a task harder than doing it.

Four years of teaching taught me that if something is hard to explain, it's usually badly designed. I try to build things the same way I used to teach: small steps, clear instructions, no one left behind.
